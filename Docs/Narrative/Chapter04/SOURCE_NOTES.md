# Chapter 4 source notes

These production references are transcribed from the locked v3 script. They support later fact-checking and legal review; they are not instructions shown to the player.

## Real case inspiration

The script states that a guzheng student in Xuzhou bought a seller-recommended hawksbill finger-pick set in March 2023, searched the unfamiliar material name after delivery, and reported the concern. The playable story remains a fictional 2025 adaptation. The friend, dialogue, order notifications, investigation montage, and handcuff staging are dramatized.

- Jiangsu Procuratorate article: https://www.jsjc.gov.cn/zl/6736/202506/t20250624_189512.shtml

## Species protection reference

The production script identifies 玳瑁, Eretmochelys imbricata, in the National Key Protected Wild Animal List as Class I protected wildlife.

- National Forestry and Grassland Administration and Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs list: https://yyj.moa.gov.cn/gzdt/202102/P020210205564727206658.pdf
