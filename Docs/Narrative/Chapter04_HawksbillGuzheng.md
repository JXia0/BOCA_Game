# Chapter 4 implementation record

## Locked source

`剧本/BOCA 游戏剧本_玳瑁古筝义甲_修改版_v3.docx`

The Unity flow implements the source's eight-scene structure and preserves these decisions:

- The teacher only reminds the player to buy reliable finger picks and is not involved in the transaction.
- Route A completes the purchase before the player understands the unfamiliar material name.
- Route B uses a friend who has already bought the set and casually says it feels good; the friend is not suspicious at first.
- Curiosity, not prior suspicion, leads both routes to research 玳瑁.
- Route A can preserve the player's order, payment, logistics, packaging, labels, and seller chat.
- Route B can preserve only the friend's shared messages, photo, and listing screenshot; it cannot access private order records.
- A seller claim and product photograph do not prove the material or a criminal offence.
- The report preserves facts, claims, sources, and uncertainty instead of generating an accusation.
- The seller packing-room sequence is an audience-only fictional cutaway and not evidence seen by the protagonist.
- The police intervention occurs months later after a separate investigation; it is not framed as an automatic result of one report or as a conviction.
- The marketplace, shop, accounts, order numbers, payment records, and addresses are fictional.

The production flow is `Assets/Resources/BOCA/Flows/chapter-04.json`. Player-facing English is `Assets/Resources/BOCA/Localization/en-US/chapter-04.json`.
