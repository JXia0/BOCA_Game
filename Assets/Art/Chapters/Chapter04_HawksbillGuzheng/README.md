- `C4_ENV_01_concert_hall` - guzheng performance stage and audience, evening
- `C4_ENV_02_bedroom_desk` - bedroom, open guzheng case, phone and computer staging
- `C4_ENV_03_package_view` - fictional package, labels and finger picks, no real order data
- `C4_ENV_04_packing_room` - cramped fictional seller workspace, order queue and packets
- `C4_ENV_05_intervention` - the same packing room months later during police intervention

## P0 portraits

- `C4_POR_00_player` - first-person player reaction portrait, neutral design
- `C4_POR_01_teacher` - calm message portrait; teacher is not involved in the purchase
- `C4_POR_02_friend` - casual and pleased, not suspicious at first
- `C4_POR_03_seller` - ordinary appearance, no exaggerated villain design
